# High-temperature oxidation of metals

Oxide scale growth on a metal surface is controlled by diffusion of metal ions outward and oxygen inward through the growing oxide layer.

## What to record

Record specimen ID, surface condition, exposure time, furnace temperature and any visible change in colour or scale morphology. Photograph the specimen before and after exposure.

## Confirming the mechanism

Scale phases can be confirmed with XRD, and cross-section morphology with SEM. Parabolic weight gain over time usually indicates diffusion-controlled oxide growth.
